# 📺 Prison Break

> *A story of loyalty, sacrifice, and intelligence in the face of injustice.*

## 
**Prison Break** follows **Michael Scofield**, a brilliant structural engineer who makes the bold choice to enter prison willingly to save his brother, **Lincoln Burrows**, who has been falsely sentenced to death. Believing that Lincoln is innocent, Michael designs a complex escape plan and hides it within a full-body tattoo containing secret codes and blueprints. His determination shows how far someone will go for family, even when the odds are impossible and the risks are life-threatening.

Inside the prison, Michael faces constant challenges and moral decisions while forming alliances with dangerous inmates and navigating a corrupt system. The escape is only the beginning — once outside, Michael and Lincoln uncover a larger conspiracy that proves the fight for freedom continues far beyond prison walls. The story blends emotional depth, strategic thinking, and intense suspense, showing that true strength comes from loyalty, intelligence, and sacrifice.
